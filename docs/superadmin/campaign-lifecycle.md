# Campaign lifecycle — 2026-09-28

- Conclude: revoke memberships for this campaign, pause it, end its contract and free its municipality. Keep campaign records. Move user-owned directory annotations into the private archive to prevent reuse in a subsequent campaign. Keep Auth users and memberships in other municipalities.
- Reactivate: require available municipality and valid expiry, restore the contact administrator only, restore private annotations. Team members must be reassigned deliberately.
- Purge: explicit municipality confirmation and acknowledgement. Delete campaign records, its archive, old delivery payloads and historical records covered by the existing purge. Keep account and other municipalities. Queue one deletion notification after the transaction succeeds. An owned Storage object blocks purge until physically removed; external backup retention is disclosed in the notification.
- Email: outbox is committed with each transition; SMTP delivery occurs afterward. Missing SMTP config never produces a false success. Pending/failed messages can be retried from Clients and municipalities. Stable Message-ID reduces accidental duplicates; SMTP cannot guarantee exactly-once delivery after a process crash. A sending status without acknowledgement requires operator review rather than automatic resending.
